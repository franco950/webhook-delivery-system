The CPU profile

At 200/s, most of the CPU is waking goroutines

30 s profile from t+28 s to t+58 s: 15.4 CPU-seconds, so 0.51 of a core. Each bar is the share of CPU spent in that kind of goroutine, including what it called.

At a slow, steady rate almost every event wakes a sleeping goroutine, and the wake-up costs CPU however little work follows. That's why the scheduler is half the profile here, against a third at 1,200/s and a quarter at 3,000/s. The runtime's own CPU estimate for the sending period is 0.36 cores, below the profile's 0.51, the same gap seen in run 56.
