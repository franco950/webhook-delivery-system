The stall at t+32.7 s

150 ms of nothing, then a burst

What the logs show: from t+32.53 s everything slowed at once. The generator fell up to 146 ms behind its trace, and completed attempts across all endpoints dropped from ~680 to ~340 per 100 ms. Because the generator catches up by sending late events immediately, ~140 ms of analytics events (about 440) arrived within a few milliseconds. That's more than the 200-slot queue holds, so 46 were refused while the dispatcher moved them into lanes. Lanes weren't the problem: analytics was at its 500-lane ceiling, with only a few dozen deliveries waiting in them.

The cause isn't fully visible. A garbage collection ran in that second, the run's longest (its stop-the-world pause added 14.6 ms) with the heap near its peak (365 MB). The CPU profiler was also recording at the time. A GC cycle at a large heap slows every goroutine through mark assists, which fits a whole-process slowdown, but the logs can't prove it accounts for all 150 ms.

What it means: any pause of more than ~70 ms fills a 200-slot queue at 3,000/s, whatever causes it. Resize pauses did this to the fraud vendor, and now a runtime pause did it to analytics. A bigger queue for the high-volume endpoints, or holding instead of refusing for "never lose" endpoints, covers both.
